Romantapedia is a companion for romantasy readers. Find out which court you belong to, follow every romance and rivalry, and trade theories with other readers, all stopped at the page you've reached.

Find your court

II

Every bond, mapped

A living web of lovers, rivals, family and allies that grows as you read.

III

Theories with readers

Post theories, comment and vote. Every post is tagged with how far into the series it spoils.

IV

Rise through the ranks

Good theories earn points, from Human all the way to High Lord or High Lady.

V

Spoiler-safe by design

Tell the app which book you're on. Characters, lore, the timeline and the map never read ahead of you.
